# Granular Certificates API: Coming soon!

## Overview

CleanCounts was the first registry in North America to pilot hourly certificates, working with Google in 2021. In 2026, CleanCounts is launching a fully EnergyTag-aligned granular certificate platform. Certificates at the Watt-hour level are informed by data validated by grid operators.

A megawatt-hour produced at noon and a megawatt-hour produced at midnight are not the same product. Buyers matching their electricity use around the clock increasingly want to know when their energy was produced relative to their own consumption. Manufacturers who export their products will increasingly face carbon border adjustment rules related to the energy inputs into their manufacturing processes. CleanCounts works so North American companies can compete globally. Generators that can show when, where, and in what year their energy was produced may earn more for their granular power in today's market.

Hydrogen producers need hourly matching to qualify for the 45V clean hydrogen tax credit. The hour is where the value is.

CleanCounts also supports NERC e-Tag capabilities across half of our operational footprint today, expanding gradually across North America, so imported and exported power can carry this important transmission attribute to validate delivery of physical power across NERC balancing authority borders.
