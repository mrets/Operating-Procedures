## Overview

A **granular certificate (GC)** is an energy attribute certificate that records *when* and *where* a unit of energy was produced, typically at **hourly** (or finer) resolution. Traditional certificates such as RECs are usually issued per MWh with a monthly or annual vintage. Granular certificates make **time-matched** (24/7) clean energy claims possible.

## Why Granularity Matters

- **Temporal matching:** Consumption can be matched to clean generation hour by hour, not just over a year.
- **Grid realism:** Hourly matching reflects when clean power is actually available on the grid.
- **Storage accounting:** Charging and discharging can be tracked so stored clean energy keeps its attributes.
- **Stronger claims:** Buyers can show that clean energy was produced when they used power.

## Annual vs. Hourly Matching

| Attribute              | Annual Matching         | Hourly (Granular) Matching   |
|------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------|
| Time resolution        | Month or year           | Hour (or sub-hour)           |
| Typical instrument     | REC / GO                | Granular certificate         |
| Claim strength         | Volumetric              | Time- and location-matched   |
| Data requirements      | Monthly meter totals    | Interval meter data          |
| Storage treatment      | Usually not tracked     | Charge/discharge tracked     |
